About Shadeswap

Our Mission

Shadeswap was founded to preserve sovereign financial privacy in an era of invasive financial surveillance. We offer zero-KYC instant cryptographic swaps directly into Monero (XMR) alongside a trustless non-custodial escrow platform for peer-to-peer merchandise and service settlement.

We charge a fixed 5% escrow protection fee on all deal settlements. Swap operations carry a 2% liquidity & processing fee. Terms Overview

Users are solely responsible for providing accurate receiving addresses. Monero swaps are irreversible once broadcast to the blockchain. Escrow funds are released to the vendor only after explicit buyer confirmation or resolution of a valid dispute.

What is Cryptographic Escrow?

An escrow is a neutral third-party holding arrangement where cryptocurrency funds are locked securely until both buyer and vendor fulfill their agreed trade commitments.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the escrow process take?

The escrow process typically takes 1-24 hours depending on the transaction complexity and verification requirements. After you send funds to our secure wallet, our team monitors confirmations on the blockchain. Once the seller confirms delivery of goods or services and you verify completion, we release the funds promptly. Most transactions complete within 3-12 hours during business hours.

What are the fees for using Shadeswap?

Our standard escrow fee is 5% of the transaction amount, which is a competitive rate for secure cryptocurrency transactions. For swap services, we charge a fixed fee of 2% per transaction. These fees cover our security infrastructure, fraud detection, customer support, and blockchain monitoring. There are no hidden fees - what you see is what you pay. Fees are deducted automatically and clearly displayed before you confirm any transaction.

Is my personal information secure?

Absolutely. We employ the highest standards of encryption and security protocols to ensure your personal information remains private and secure. All data is stored on encrypted servers with multi-layer protection. We use industry-standard SSL encryption for all communications, and we never share your information with third parties without your explicit consent. We're committed to privacy-first operations and maintain compliance with international data protection standards.

Which cryptocurrencies can I swap to Monero?

Currently, we support swapping Bitcoin (BTC), Litecoin (LTC), Ethereum (ETH), and Tether (USDT on Ethereum/ERC-20) to Monero (XMR). We're continuously evaluating additional cryptocurrency options based on user demand. Bitcoin and Monero swaps are our most popular service due to the privacy benefits. Litecoin and Ethereum swaps offer good speeds and lower fees compared to Bitcoin. USDT swaps provide stablecoin convenience for those seeking price predictability.

How do I track my transaction?

Each transaction is assigned a unique ID (starting with "SW-" or "ES-"). You can use this ID when contacting our support team for updates. Additionally, you will receive email notifications at each major stage of the process - when payment is received, when the counterparty confirms, and when funds are released. You can also save your transaction ID to check status anytime. Contact our support team by email for transaction updates.

What happens if there's a dispute?

If a dispute arises, contact our support team immediately with your transaction ID. We have a professional dispute resolution process where our team reviews all evidence including transaction details, communications between parties, and supporting documentation provided by both sides. We investigate thoroughly and aim to resolve disputes fairly within 3-7 business days. Both parties have the opportunity to present their case. Our decision is binding and we make every effort to ensure fair outcomes based on the agreed transaction terms.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the escrow service work?

Our escrow service acts as a trusted third party that holds funds until both parties fulfill their obligations. The process involves the buyer sending funds to our secure wallet, the seller delivering the goods or services, and then the buyer confirming receipt, at which point we release the funds to the seller. This protects both parties - the buyer knows funds are safe until delivery, and the seller knows payment is guaranteed. Our platform manages the entire transaction securely with encrypted communications and blockchain verification.

Are there minimum or maximum transaction limits?

The minimum amount for escrow transactions is 0.0001 BTC (approximately $6-8 USD depending on market rates). For swaps, the minimum varies slightly by cryptocurrency but is generally around $10-15 USD equivalent. The maximum limit is $50,000 USD for escrow transactions and $80,000 USD for swaps. Transactions exceeding $10,000 require manual coordination via support@shadeswap.ink for liquidity reservation.

What happens if there's a dispute?

In case of a dispute, our team will review all transaction details, communications between parties, and any provided evidence. We aim to resolve disputes fairly and promptly within 3-7 business days. Both parties will have the opportunity to present their case before a final decision is made. We examine documentation, timestamps, and any supporting materials to make an informed decision. If necessary, the funds remain in escrow during the investigation. Our goal is always to reach a fair resolution based on the original transaction agreement.

Do you require KYC verification?

We maintain a privacy-first approach and do not require KYC (Know Your Customer) verification for most transactions under $10,000 USD equivalent. However, larger transactions may require basic identity verification to comply with anti-money laundering regulations. You can use our services as a guest without creating an account. This flexibility, combined with our security protocols, makes us popular for those seeking privacy without compromising safety.

Can I cancel a transaction?

You can cancel a transaction only before the seller begins fulfilling the agreement. If you've already sent funds and the transaction is in progress, you cannot cancel it without mutual agreement from the other party. If the counterparty has not taken action within 48 hours, contact our support team and we may be able to release your funds. Once a swap is initiated and funds are sent to the address, the transaction cannot be reversed due to the nature of blockchain transactions.

What cryptocurrencies and networks do you support?

We support Bitcoin (BTC) on the main Bitcoin network, Litecoin (LTC), Ethereum (ETH) on ERC-20, USDT on Ethereum (ERC-20), and Monero (XMR). For swaps, all supported cryptocurrencies can be converted to Monero. For escrow services, you can use any of these cryptocurrencies. We're continuously monitoring blockchain networks for additional integration opportunities based on user demand and security considerations.
